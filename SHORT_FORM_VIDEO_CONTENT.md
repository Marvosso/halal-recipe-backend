## On-Screen Text Best Practices

### Text Placement
- **Top 1/3:** Main hook or question
- **Middle:** Key information or steps
- **Bottom:** CTA or app name

### Text Style
- **Bold, large font** (minimum 24pt)
- **High contrast** (white text on dark background or vice versa)
- **Short phrases** (3-5 words max per line)
- **Timed to visuals** (appear/disappear with action)

### Text Animation
- **Fade in/out** for smooth transitions
- **Pop in** for emphasis
- **Slide in** for step-by-step content
- **Typewriter effect** for dramatic reveals

## Production Tips

### Equipment
- **Phone camera** (iPhone 13+ or equivalent)
- **Ring light** for consistent lighting
- **Tripod** for stable shots
- **External microphone** (optional, for voiceovers)

### Editing
- **CapCut** (free, TikTok-friendly)
- **InShot** (mobile-friendly)
- **Adobe Premiere Rush** (advanced)

### Best Practices
- **Film in vertical format** (9:16 aspect ratio)
- **Keep it under 30 seconds** (TikTok algorithm favors shorter)
- **Add captions** (many watch without sound)
- **Use trending audio** when relevant
- **Post consistently** (daily or 3-5x per week)
